Subscription terms

Tunebox Plus is a monthly subscription. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period at the then-current price unless you cancel.

How to cancel

To cancel, sign in, open Account, choose Membership, then select End membership. You must cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

Cancelling during the free trial ends access immediately. The activation fee is not charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

Refunds

Payments are non-refundable, and we do not provide refunds or credits for partial billing periods.
